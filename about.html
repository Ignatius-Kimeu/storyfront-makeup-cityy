Meet O'Rnella.

"Allow me the ability to aspire and inspire, one beautiful face at a time."

O'Rnella Nthambi is a Nairobi-based makeup artist, content creator and beauty educator, and the founder of Makeup Cityy. What started as one artist building a name has grown into three things at once: a glam and bridal makeup practice, a beauty academy training the next generation of Kenyan MUAs, and a content platform followed by tens of thousands of people who trust her eye.

Makeup Cityy has been named among Kenya's top 10 bridal makeup artists — recognition earned the same way the client list was: face by face.
